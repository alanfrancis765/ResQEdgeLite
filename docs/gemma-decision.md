# Gemma On-Device Feasibility Decision

- Phone RAM: 8 GB (Android 15). Above the ~6 GB guideline for Gemma 4 E2B.
- Note: realme phones can show virtual RAM expansion. Confirm the physical RAM in Settings > About phone before relying on the 8 GB figure.
- Decision: the SOS form stays the primary input. Gemma is feasible on this phone, so try it as a stretch goal after the mesh works.
